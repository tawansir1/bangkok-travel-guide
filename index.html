What to eat

Street food prices. Eat where the locals queue.

  • Pad Thai ผัดไทย60–100 THB

    Stir-fried rice noodles with egg, tofu, shrimp and peanuts. Squeeze the lime over it.

  • Khao Man Gai ข้าวมันไก่50–80 THB

    Poached chicken on rice cooked in chicken stock, with a ginger and soybean sauce.

  • Som Tam ส้มตำ50–80 THB

    Green papaya salad: sour, sweet, salty and hot. Say “phet nit noi” for a little spice.

  • Mango Sticky Rice ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง80–150 THB

    Ripe mango with sticky rice and salted coconut cream. Best from April to June.

Good to know

What should I wear to temples?

Cover your shoulders and knees, and take your shoes off before you enter a temple hall.

When is the best time to visit?

November to February is cool and dry. March to May is very hot, and June to October brings afternoon rain.

Which card do I need for the train?

BTS uses its own Rabbit card or single tickets. MRT takes contactless bank cards or tokens. They are separate systems, so you pay again when you change between them.

Useful Thai phrases

Sawasdee krub / ka hello. Khob khun krub / ka thank you. Tao rai? how much? Mai phet not spicy. Men end with “krub”, women with “ka”.

Local etiquette

Respect the monarchy, don’t touch people’s heads, and don’t point your feet at people or Buddha images.
