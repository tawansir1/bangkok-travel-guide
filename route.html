How the fares work

Each operator charges separately, so changing from BTS to MRT means paying twice. Changing between the two BTS lines at Siam does not.

LineFareHow it is charged
BTS Skytrain17–47 THBBy stations in the city section. Extensions are 17–45 THB extra, capped at 65 THB per trip.
MRT Blue Line17–44 THBBy stations, new table from 3 July 2026.
Airport Rail Link15–45 THBBy stations. Airport to Phaya Thai is 45 THB.
Gold Line17 THBFlat fare. Connects Krung Thon Buri to ICONSIAM.
Express boat19 THBOrange flag, flat fare. Pay the conductor on board.
